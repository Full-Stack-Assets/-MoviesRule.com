The PlayStation blog just unveiled the Marvel‑inspired Wolverine Battle Yellow PS5 Edition Bundle, a limited‑run console that mirrors the ferocity of Logan. The bundle pairs a yellow‑finished PS5 Digital Edition with a claw‑stamped DualSense controller and includes a game code for the upcoming Marvel’s Wolverine title. Designed in collaboration with Insomniac Games, Marvel Games, and artist Jock, the hardware showcases exposed claw marks and a peek‑under‑hood view. Because the bundle ships globally as a limited edition, it could become a collector’s item for both gamers and comic fans.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Wolverine PS5 bundle blends iconic comic aesthetics with next‑gen hardware, making it a must‑have for fans.</Callout>

## What happened
At San Diego Comic‑Con, Insomniac and Marvel revealed details of the Wolverine Battle Yellow design. The PS5 Digital Edition features a bold yellow finish, exposed claw‑mark graphics, and a transparent panel that shows internal components. The matching DualSense controller is wrapped in the same yellow hue with slash‑like accents that echo Wolverine’s adamantium claws.

The bundle also includes a digital code for Marvel’s Wolverine, the upcoming action‑adventure from Insomniac, and the controller is sold separately for those who already own a PS5. Availability is global, with pre‑orders opening shortly after the announcement.

## Why it matters
The collaboration underscores Sony’s strategy of leveraging blockbuster franchises to refresh console sales and attract collectors. By tying a high‑profile Marvel character to a limited‑edition hardware run, the bundle creates buzz that can boost both PS5 hardware demand and the launch of the Wolverine game. For fans, the themed design offers a tangible way to express fandom, while the scarcity factor can drive secondary‑market activity and brand loyalty.
