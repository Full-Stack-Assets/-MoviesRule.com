A Massachusetts State Police trooper has been suspended with pay after being charged with stealing a Bobcat excavator from a contractor. The trooper, Joseph Franklin, is facing felony charges in connection with the alleged larceny. This incident raises concerns about the actions of law enforcement officials and the consequences they face when accused of wrongdoing.

   ## What happened
   Trooper Joseph Franklin is accused of stealing a Bobcat T-190 loader after claiming that the contractor didn’t complete the work on his property. Franklin told Palmer Police that he had paid the contractor $11,000 to clean up trees from a severe thunderstorm. However, when the work wasn’t completed, his attorney apparently told him to hold onto the Bobcat as collateral. 
   The incident report states that Franklin was informed that holding onto the property without a court order would be considered larceny. Despite this, Franklin continued to possess the Bobcat, leading to his suspension with pay. 
   The Massachusetts State Police have faced similar incidents in the past, including the suspension of four troopers charged in connection with the death of a recruit.
