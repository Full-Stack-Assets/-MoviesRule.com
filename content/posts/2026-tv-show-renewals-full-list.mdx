The 2026‑2027 TV season is shaping up with a flood of renewal announcements across broadcast, cable, and streaming platforms. Networks have already locked in schedules for the fall, while streaming services revealed return dates for many of their flagship series. From long‑running dramas like Grey’s Anatomy to reality staples such as The Amazing Race, dozens of titles are confirmed to continue into 2026 and beyond. This wave of renewals gives fans a clear roadmap of what to binge and helps advertisers plan their inventory. Understanding which shows are staying power versus seasonal experiments is essential for anyone tracking television trends.

## What happened
Screen Rant compiled a list of every series that has received an official renewal for the 2026 season. The list spans major broadcast networks, cable channels, and the biggest streaming platforms, confirming that the industry has already set a solid slate for the year.

Highlights include Abbott Elementary moving into its sixth season, Grey’s Anatomy entering season 23, and The Walking Dead: Dead City slated for a third run. Reality heavyweights such as The Amazing Race (season 39) and Dancing With the Stars (season 35) also secured extensions, while genre‑bending titles like American Horror Story (season 13) and It’s Always Sunny in Philadelphia (season 18) continue their long runs.

While most shows have confirmed season numbers, exact premiere dates remain undisclosed for many, especially those shifting to mid‑season slots.

## Why it matters
The breadth of renewals signals confidence from networks and streaming services in their existing franchises, reducing the risk of abrupt cancellations that can alienate viewers. Advertisers gain a clearer picture of where to allocate spend, as long‑running series often deliver consistent ratings and subscriber retention. For fans, the confirmed returns eliminate the guesswork around cliffhangers and allow for better planning of watchlists across platforms.

## How to think about it
Treat the renewal list as a baseline for strategic planning: prioritize binge‑watching long‑running dramas if you crave narrative continuity, and keep an eye on reality competition slots for weekly appointment viewing.
