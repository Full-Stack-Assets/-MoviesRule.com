Latin Quarter Distribution announced it has secured worldwide sales rights to José Dell’Acqua’s new Uruguayan crime thriller ‘Maltrato Animal.’ The film, still in the editing stage, was showcased at Locarno’s First Look competition, where the deal was finalized. Set against Uruguay’s 2002 economic crisis, the story follows a struggling veterinarian who turns to illegal breeding and drug peddling to survive. The acquisition gives the film a clear path to international festivals and streaming platforms, raising its profile beyond Uruguay’s modest market.

## What happened
Latin Quarter Distribution confirmed the purchase of world sales rights after a rough‑cut screening at Locarno’s First Look focus, a segment highlighting emerging talent from Uruguay. The film, directed by José Dell’Acqua and produced with German Ormaechea, was shot over four years, employing an all‑Uruguayan cast and crew, and is currently in post‑production. The narrative reflects the 2002 financial crisis, portraying a veterinarian forced into a clandestine network of illegal breeding and pharmaceutical trafficking.
