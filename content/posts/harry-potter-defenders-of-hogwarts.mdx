The Harry Potter franchise continues to expand with a new board game, Defenders of Hogwarts, set to launch in October 2026. Created by MinaLima, this game was initially a Kickstarter campaign that exceeded its goal. Fans can now pre-order the game in various bundles. The game's release is significant as it offers a new way for fans to engage with the magical world of Harry Potter. 
   <Callout type="takeaway">Defenders of Hogwarts is an asymmetric deckbuilding adventure where players can defend Hogwarts or join the Dark Forces.</Callout>
   ## What happened
   Defenders of Hogwarts is a board game where players can choose to defend Hogwarts or join the Dark Forces. The game is designed for 2-5 players and takes up to 90 minutes to complete. It includes over 200 components, such as illustrated cards and props. The game's Kickstarter campaign was highly successful, exceeding its goal of £50,000 and raising £1,269,635.
   The game offers different pledge options, including The Defender, The Guardian, and The Collector, which cost £58, £115, and £280, respectively. Each bundle includes the standard board game and additional items, such as a Protective Card Sleeve Set and a Deluxe Box Sleeve.
   The game's release is a result of the ongoing popularity of the Harry Potter franchise, which has seen recent releases such as the video game Hogwarts Legacy and an upcoming television show.
