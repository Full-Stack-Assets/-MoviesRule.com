A heat advisory has been announced for Thursday’s Leagues Cup 2026 match between the Portland Timbers and Club Puebla at Providence Park. The advisory allows fans to bring sealed sports drinks and water bottles into the stadium, a departure from the usual policy. A cooling station will be set up near Gate D in the Guest Services Center to help spectators stay comfortable. The league’s decision reflects growing concerns about player and fan safety during extreme temperatures.

<Callout type="takeaway">Fans can stay hydrated and cool thanks to relaxed drink rules and a new on‑site cooling station.</Callout>

## What happened
The league issued a heat advisory for the Thursday evening match at 7:30 p.m. PT, permitting fans to bring sealed sports drinks and sealed water bottles into Providence Park. The Guest Services Center near Gate D will host a cooling station where attendees can refill bottles and seek shade.

Fans can locate the cooling station and additional water‑filling points using PTFCmap.com, which maps all amenities relative to seat locations. The advisory is part of the league’s broader heat‑protocol guidelines aimed at reducing heat‑related risks for both players and spectators.

## Why it matters
Allowing sealed beverages directly addresses dehydration risks that can impair fan enjoyment and, in extreme cases, health. The cooling station provides a centralized spot for rapid temperature relief, potentially reducing heat‑related incidents during a high‑profile Leagues Cup fixture. By adapting stadium policy, the league signals a proactive stance on climate‑driven challenges, setting a precedent for other venues facing similar summer conditions.

## How to think about it
Plan to arrive early so you can locate Gate D and the cooling station before the crowd builds. Bring a sealed bottle that you can refill, and consider a lightweight hat or portable fan for added shade. Monitor your own temperature and watch for signs of heat exhaustion, especially if you’ll be standing for long periods.

<Callout type="warning">Do not bring open containers or large coolers, as they will be denied entry under the advisory.</Callout>
