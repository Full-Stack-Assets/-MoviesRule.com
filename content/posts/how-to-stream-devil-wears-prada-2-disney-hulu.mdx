The fashion‑forward sequel 'The Devil Wears Prada 2' is finally online, arriving on Disney+ and Hulu early Wednesday morning. After a two‑decade gap, Meryl Streep, Anne Hathaway, Emily Blunt and Stanley Tucci reprise their iconic roles for a new chapter in runway drama. The film premiered at 3 a.m. ET (midnight PT) on July 29, 2026, making it instantly accessible to subscribers. For fans of the original, the release offers a chance to revisit Miranda Priestly’s empire and see how the magazine navigates a digital age. Because the title is bundled with Disney’s streaming plans, understanding the cost and access details matters.

## What happened
The sequel premiered on Disney+ and Hulu on Wednesday, July 29, 2026, becoming available at 3 a.m. ET (12 a.m. PT). The original cast—Meryl Streep, Anne Hathaway, Emily Blunt and Stanley Tucci—return, joined by newcomers Justin Theroux, Lucy Liu, Kenneth Branagh and Simone Ashley.

Disney+ and Hulu promote a combined subscription bundle starting at $11.99 per month, giving access to both services and the new film.

Set twenty years after the 2006 original, the story follows Andy Sachs helping Miranda Priestly steer Runway magazine through a shifting media landscape and corporate threats.

## Why it matters
Streaming the sequel on two major platforms expands its reach beyond theatrical windows, allowing a new generation to experience the franchise while generating subscription revenue for Disney. The bundled pricing makes the offering attractive for households already invested in Disney’s ecosystem, but it also reinforces the paywall for viewers without a subscription.
