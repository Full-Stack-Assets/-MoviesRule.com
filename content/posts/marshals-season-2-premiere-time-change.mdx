The long‑awaited second season of CBS’s western‑action series Marshals is finally set to return. The premiere is scheduled for Sunday, October 4, 2026, but CBS has moved the start time to 8:30 PM ET instead of its usual 8 PM slot. The shift is a one‑night adjustment to accommodate an NFL double‑header and the network’s 60 Minutes lead‑in. After the first episode, the series will revert to its regular 8 PM ET slot beginning October 11. Fans can also catch new episodes on Paramount+ the day they air.

<Callout type="takeaway">Marshals season 2 launches on Oct 4, 2026 at 8:30 PM ET, then returns to its regular 8 PM slot the following week.</Callout>

## What happened
CBS confirmed that Marshals will return on Sunday, October 4, 2026, with Luke Grimes reprising his role as Kayce Dutton. Production on the second season began in June in Park City, Utah, and the crew was spotted on location throughout the summer. The network announced the premiere will air at 8:30 PM ET, a half‑hour later than the series’ standard slot.

The time shift is tied to CBS’s fall lineup, which opens the evening with an NFL double‑header followed by 60 Minutes at 7:30 PM ET. Marshals will take the 8:30 PM slot, and the Season 4 premiere of Tracker will close the night at 9:30 PM ET. Starting the following week on October 11, Marshals moves back to its usual 8 PM ET slot, with Tracker at 9 PM.

All new episodes will be available the same night on Paramount+, allowing subscribers to stream the show if they miss the live broadcast.

## Why it matters
The scheduling tweak highlights how major networks balance sports, news, and scripted programming to maximize viewership. By giving the NFL double‑header a clear lead‑in, CBS hopes to retain sports audiences for the drama that follows. For Marshals fans, the half‑hour delay on premiere night is a minor inconvenience, but the quick return to the regular slot minimizes disruption. The simultaneous Paramount+ release reflects the growing importance of streaming as a safety net for viewers who cannot tune in live.
