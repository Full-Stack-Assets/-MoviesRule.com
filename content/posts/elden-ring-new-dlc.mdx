Elden Ring is set to receive new DLC this month, adding to the game's already vast content. The game has continued to win awards and receive updates since its release in 2022. FromSoftware has announced that the new DLC will be available across all platforms, including the Switch 2, where Elden Ring is set to release in late August.

<Callout type="takeaway">Elden Ring's new DLC will increase available content and give fans a reason to return to the game.</Callout>

## What happened
The new DLC is scheduled to launch soon and will include brand-new content for players to enjoy. FromSoftware has also announced that the DLC being introduced in the Nintendo Switch 2 version of the game, Tarnished Edition, will be available to players on other platforms as The Tarnished Pack DLC. This will include Torrent skins and brand-new armor sets.

FromSoftware has been working on the game's updates and expansions since its release. The company has been listening to fan feedback and has been releasing updates to improve the gaming experience. The new DLC is a significant addition to the game and will give fans a new reason to play.

The Tarnished Pack DLC will be available for a small fee, which is roughly $3.50. This is a relatively small amount for a piece of DLC, especially considering the game's age.

## Why it matters
The new DLC is significant because it shows that FromSoftware is still committed to supporting Elden Ring. The game has been out for nearly half a decade, but the company is still releasing new content and updates. This is a testament to the game's popularity and the company's dedication to its fans.
