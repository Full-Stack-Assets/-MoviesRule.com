Indie cinema gets a fresh boost as two well‑known screen veterans join forces in Amanda Lipitz’s first narrative feature, Nice Eggs. Jane Krakowski, famed for 30 Rock, and rising star Angourie Rice completed the New Jersey shoot this week, confirming the film is on track for a 2027 festival debut. The drama follows a small‑town young woman navigating a turbulent mother‑daughter relationship while chasing her writing dreams. With Lipitz’s reputation for emotionally resonant storytelling, the project could become a standout coming‑of‑age entry on the streaming circuit. Audiences and distributors are already eyeing its potential for awards season.
<Callout type="takeaway">Nice Eggs pairs seasoned comedy talent with emerging drama chops, signaling a promising indie debut for director Amanda Lipitz.</Callout>
## What happened
The production wrapped its New Jersey schedule last Friday, with both leads confirming the shoot ran smoothly despite the typical indie budget constraints. The film is produced by Key to the City Productions alongside Henry Tisch, Diane Nabatoff, Tory Metzger, and Sandi Farkas.
Nice Eggs centers on Cori Mulligan, played by Rice, a small‑town writer‑in‑training who must break free from her erratic mother Ellen, portrayed by Krakowski, and the generational patterns that bind them. Lipitz told Deadline that the story explores mothers and daughters, invisible inheritance, and how early experiences echo throughout life.
