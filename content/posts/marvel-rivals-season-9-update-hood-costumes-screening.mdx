Marvel Rivals just kicked off Season 9 with a major content drop that adds the notorious villain The Hood to its roster. The update, live from August 7, rolls out new lore entries, three fresh costume bundles, and a limited‑time Times Square screening of Captain America clips. It also brings performance optimizations across PC and consoles and a new Twitch Drops lineup. For players and streaming fans, the changes promise fresh cosmetics, new gameplay strategies, and extra viewing events.
<Callout type="takeaway">Season 9 expands Marvel Rivals with new hero content, cosmetic bundles, and cross‑media events.</Callout>
## What happened
The August 7 update introduces The Hood lore titled “Carved in Stone” and a new Moon Knight lore “Faces of the Moon.” It also adds several PYO bundles: The Hood – Sinful Blackout Bundle, Iron Fist – Furious Flow Bundle, Moon Knight – Fist of Khonshu Bundle, plus individual items like The Hood – Oblivion's Whisper and various emoji bundles. Players can mix and match costumes and emojis, with the bundle price automatically adjusting to match the content.
In addition to cosmetics, Marvel Rivals hosts a Times Square screening of Captain America: The Winter Soldier clips, granting an exclusive spray after a two‑minute view. Twitch Drops run from August 7 to September 4, offering the Elsa Bloodstone – Will of Galacta costume. The patch also implements texture compression, shaving roughly 30% off PC install size and improving console footprints, while fixing the Peni Parker visual bug.
## Why it matters
The new villain expands team‑based PvP strategies, forcing players to adapt to The Hood’s abilities and reshaping meta compositions. Cosmetic bundles deepen personalization, encouraging micro‑transactions while keeping pricing transparent. Cross‑media events like the Times Square screening bridge gaming and movie fandoms, driving engagement for both the game and the Marvel film catalog. Performance gains lower barriers for new players on lower‑end hardware, potentially expanding the player base.
