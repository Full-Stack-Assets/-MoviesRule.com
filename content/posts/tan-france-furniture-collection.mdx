Tan France, star of Netflix’s 'Queer Eye' and 'Next in Fashion,' has made his debut into furniture with the Castlery x Tan France Collection. The collection includes 18 pieces that blend France’s personal, multicultural perspective with Castlery’s focus on craftsmanship, materiality, and accessibility. France’s interest in interiors deepened while building his dream home, a Salt Lake City Tudor estate, with his husband, Rob. The media personality was approached by Singapore-based furniture Castlery after they called his agent. France was given the freedom to design whatever he wanted, which was a major plus point for him. The collection is emblematic of France’s background, with elements reflecting his combined South Asian and British heritage. <Callout type="takeaway">Tan France's furniture collection with Castlery marks a new venture for the Netflix star, blending his personal style with the brand's focus on craftsmanship.</Callout> ## What happened Tan France was approached by Castlery to design a furniture collection. The collection includes 18 pieces, spanning living, dining, lighting, rugs, decor, and accessories. Key pieces include the Anika table lamp, the Kagan sofa, and the Avan Dining Collection. The collection reflects France’s combined South Asian and British heritage, with elements referencing architectural and visual motifs common to both regions. The Anika table lamp and Rayan mirror, for example, reference classic South Asian doorway arches with their curved and scalloped shapes.
