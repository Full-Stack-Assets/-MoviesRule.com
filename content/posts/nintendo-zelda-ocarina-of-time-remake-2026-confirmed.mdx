Nintendo just used its quarterly earnings call to reassure fans that the long‑awaited Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake remains on schedule. The company listed the title under “late 2026” in its financial report and reiterated that it will launch this year. This brief update comes after months of speculation about possible delays and a rumored November 11 launch. For gamers, the confirmation clears up uncertainty and lets them plan their purchase and holiday gaming calendar.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Zelda Ocarina of Time remake is still slated for a late‑2026 launch.</Callout>

## What happened
Nintendo’s most recent financial report to investors included a slide of the Switch 2 first‑party lineup, where the Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake appeared under the “late 2026” heading. In the accompanying earnings call, a Nintendo spokesperson said, “The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is also scheduled to be released this year,” confirming the title has not been pushed out of the calendar.

The update also coincided with an ESRB rating reveal, which many interpret as a sign that the game is nearing final certification. Rumors have floated a November 11 launch based on leaked amiibo listings, but Nintendo has not officially endorsed that specific date.

While Nintendo remains tight‑lipped about exact timing, the financial filing is the first concrete indication that the remake will arrive before the end of 2026.

## Why it matters
Fans have been waiting over a decade for a modern remake of the seminal 1998 title, and the lack of a firm date has fueled endless speculation. A confirmed late‑2026 window gives consumers confidence to budget for the purchase and aligns the game with the anticipated Switch 2 hardware cycle, potentially driving early‑adopter sales. Investors also view the announcement as a positive signal for Nintendo’s pipeline, reinforcing confidence in the company’s ability to deliver flagship experiences on schedule.

## How to think about it
Treat the “late 2026” label as a provisional target rather than a hard deadline. Keep an eye on Nintendo’s official channels for a precise launch announcement, especially as the year progresses and the ESRB rating process concludes. If you’re budgeting for a new console or game bundle, plan for a Q4 release window but avoid pre‑ordering until a specific date is confirmed. Consider the remake’s role in your broader gaming calendar—pairing it with other upcoming Switch 2 titles can maximize value.
