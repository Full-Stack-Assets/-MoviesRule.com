Fans of the chocolate‑loving universe have been glued to headlines about Timothée Chalamet’s next move, and the question of a Wonka sequel has dominated the conversation. Recent reporting shows conflicting statements: a Variety cover story suggested the sequel was off the table, while both the actor and Warner Bros. deny that claim. Writer‑director Simon Farnaby initially said the project was "delayed indefinitely," but later clarified his comments on Twitter, emphasizing that a script exists and the team is still committed. The uncertainty matters because the film’s future will shape the studio’s slate and Chalamet’s career trajectory.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Wonka sequel is delayed, not dead, as creators sort schedules.</Callout>

## What happened
Simon Farnaby, who co‑wrote the first Wonka film, was quoted as saying the sequel was "delayed indefinitely," a line that sparked speculation that the project might be shelved. A Warner Bros. representative pushed back, stating that reports of a delay were "entirely false" and that the creative team was hard at work on the next chapter.

Farnaby later took to Twitter to clarify his earlier remarks, writing that his quotes had been taken out of context. He affirmed, "I 100% did not say Wonka 2 wasn’t happening. A sequel was always the plan. Everyone is working really hard on it. WB are committed. There is a great script by Paul and I. Timmy wants to do it (like I said!) it’s just about working out schedules and making sure it’s as fantastic as we know it can be - and these things take a little time."

## Why it matters
The sequel’s status influences Warner Bros.’ upcoming release calendar and signals how studios handle star‑driven projects when schedules clash. For Chalamet, a successful follow‑up could cement his versatility in blockbuster territory, while a prolonged delay risks losing momentum built from his recent hits.
