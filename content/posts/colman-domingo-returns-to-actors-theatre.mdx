Oscar nominee Colman Domingo is returning to the Actors Theatre of Louisville for a special, one-night-only screening and conversation. The event, titled "A Special Evening with Colman Domingo", will feature a screening of his film Sing Sing, followed by an intimate discussion. This reunion marks a significant moment for the theatre, as Domingo has deep roots in the Louisville arts community. He has previously written and directed productions at the theatre, including the Humana Festival world premiere of Dot and August Wilson’s Seven Guitars. The event is expected to sell out quickly due to limited capacity.

   ## What happened
   The event is scheduled for Saturday, August 15, in the Pamela Brown Auditorium and is part of the Bingham Signature Shakespeare Series. It will feature a screening of Sing Sing, a film based on a true story, where Domingo plays Divine G, a wrongfully incarcerated man who finds purpose through a prison theatre program. The live post-screening discussion will explore the film's core themes of redemption, community, and the transformative power of art.
   The film features a cast of formerly incarcerated performers playing versions of themselves, adding to the authenticity of the story. As an executive producer, Domingo was heavily involved in the production of the film.
   The event is a significant moment for the theatre, as it marks a reunion with an alumnus who has had a lasting impact on the institution.
