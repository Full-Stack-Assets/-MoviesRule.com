The footage, set in a dimly lit prison‑style cage, highlights a CGI‑heavy version of the character that many viewers describe as “cursed.” While the scene is brief, the visual presentation has already sparked debate among fans about the movie’s effects direction. The reaction matters because Blanka’s design will influence how audiences receive the film’s overall aesthetic.

## What happened
The clip released by Kotaku features Momoa’s Blanka confronting Andrew Koji’s Ryu in a shadowy cage match. The character’s green skin and electric aura are rendered with a blend of motion‑capture and computer‑generated imagery, resulting in a look that appears both realistic and unsettling.

Because the scene is poorly lit, details such as texture and facial expression become harder to read, amplifying the uncanny effect. The film, scheduled for theatrical release on October 16, has already introduced an eclectic cast that includes rapper 50 Cent and other internet personalities, raising expectations for a bold visual style.

Fans of the franchise, casual moviegoers, and industry analysts alike will watch the film’s effects closely, using Blanka as a barometer for the overall production quality.

## How to think about it
When evaluating early footage, separate the actor’s performance from the visual effects pipeline. Use the clip as a data point, not a verdict, and weigh it against the film’s overall narrative promise, cast chemistry, and the studio’s track record with similar adaptations.
