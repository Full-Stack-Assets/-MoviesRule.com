Superhero films have dominated the box office for decades, but not every caped adventure earns lasting acclaim. Collider recently published its definitive ranking of the ten best superhero movies ever made, deliberately mixing blockbusters with hidden gems. For fans and streamers alike, the list offers a roadmap to the genre’s most influential works.
<Callout type="takeaway">The list shows that timeless storytelling and bold direction often outweigh franchise hype in defining the best superhero movies.</Callout>
## What happened
Collider’s editorial team assembled a list of ten superhero films, aiming for variety beyond the dominant Marvel Avengers and Spider‑Verse franchises. The ranking was based on cultural impact, critical reception, and lasting influence, and it deliberately includes titles from different eras and studios.
The top spots feature Tim Burton’s gothic “Batman Returns” (1992), praised for its distinctive visual style and Danny Elfman’s score, and Richard Donner’s pioneering “Superman” (1978), which proved a superhero could anchor a four‑quadrant blockbuster. Other entries span from the gritty realism of “Logan” to the animated innovation of “Spider‑Man: Into the Spider‑Verse”.
