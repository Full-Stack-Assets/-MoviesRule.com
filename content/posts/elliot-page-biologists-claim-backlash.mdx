Elliot Page, the Oscar‑winning trans actor, recently asserted that biologists have lied to everyone. The remark was posted in a short video that The Chronicle shared on its website. Within hours, the clip generated a flood of comments and social‑media posts condemning the claim. The episode highlights how celebrity statements can quickly become flashpoints in debates over science and identity.

## What happened
The Chronicle uploaded a video on August 6, 2026 in which Page said biologists have misled the public about fundamental facts. The clip was accompanied by a brief caption summarizing the claim.

Within the next day, users on platforms such as Twitter and Facebook posted criticism, accusing Page of spreading misinformation and undermining scientific credibility. Several science communicators responded, emphasizing the importance of evidence‑based dialogue.

## Why it matters
Because biologists are trusted sources on topics ranging from evolution to public health, challenges to their integrity can erode public trust. The backlash also intersects with trans representation, as critics sometimes conflate the actor’s gender identity with the content of the statement, complicating respectful discourse.
