Josh Hartnett stars in Fight or Flight, a movie about a plane full of assassins. The film follows a disgraced former Secret Service agent who must identify and capture an elusive hacker on a flight from Bangkok to San Francisco. Complicating matters is the fact that the commercial flight is also filled with assassins from around the world, all tasked with killing both the hacker and the agent. Now that it's streaming on Prime Video, Fight or Flight is a must-watch.

   ## What happened
   Fight or Flight was released as a Sky Cinema Original and is now streaming on Prime Video. The film is directed by James Madigan in his feature debut, after working second unit on films like Snake Eyes and The Meg. The script, by Brooks McLaren and D.J. Cotrona, appeared on the 2020 Black List of best unproduced screenplays.
   The film makes great use of a single location in a similar way to Bullet Train. In fact, this is in many ways Bullet Train but in the air, and that is in no way a criticism. The script is economical in its storytelling, but quite effective.
