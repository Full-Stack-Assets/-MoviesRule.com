The iconic cybernetic law enforcer RoboCop is stepping back into the spotlight this winter. Sideshow and Hollywood Collectibles Group have opened pre‑orders for a life‑size fiberglass helmet replica priced at $500, with shipping slated between October and December 2026. At the same time, Amazon MGM Studios confirmed a new live‑action RoboCop series for Prime Video, marking the franchise’s first major TV venture in years. Both moves signal a renewed investment in the character’s legacy and give fans tangible and streaming ways to engage.
<Callout type="takeaway">RoboCop fans can now own a museum‑grade helmet and anticipate a fresh series on Prime Video this winter.</Callout>
## What happened
The replica, marketed as the RoboCop Helmet Life‑Size Prop Replica, is crafted from premium fiberglass and features multi‑layered metallic paint that reproduces the silver, blue and purple sheens seen on screen. It includes real lenses, a custom display base, and is officially licensed, but carries a $500 price tag. Pre‑orders began this week on the Sideshow website, with an estimated delivery window of October‑December 2026.
In parallel, Amazon MGM Studios announced that a live‑action RoboCop television series has been greenlit for Prime Video. While plot details remain under wraps, the series represents the first major streaming adaptation of the franchise since the original films, and it will likely draw both longtime fans and new viewers.
## Why it matters
The helmet gives collectors a high‑fidelity piece of cinematic history, reinforcing the market for premium movie props as memorabilia. Meanwhile, the Prime Video series could revitalize the brand, expanding its audience beyond collectors to the broader streaming market and potentially influencing future film or TV projects.
