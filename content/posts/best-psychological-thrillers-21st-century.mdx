## What happened
On July 29 2026, Collider published an article titled "25 Best Psychological Thrillers of the 21st Century". The piece defines a psychological thriller as a film where internal conflict drives the tension, even when action elements are present, and it explicitly excludes movies released in the year 2000 because that year belongs to the 20th century according to the article’s criteria.
The ranking places M. Night Shyamalan’s 'Split' (2016) at the top, praising James McAvoy’s performance and its twist‑heavy narrative. Other notable entries include Michael Haneke’s 'Caché' (2005), Darren Aronofsky’s 'Black Swan' (2010), David Fincher’s 'Gone Girl' (2014), and the recent 'The Night House' (2020). Each entry is accompanied by a brief rationale that ties the film’s psychological depth to its thriller mechanics.
## Why it matters
