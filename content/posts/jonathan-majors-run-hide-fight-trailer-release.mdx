The trailer’s violent, politically charged logline has already ignited heated debate online. The release comes amid reports of on‑set safety issues and a labor dispute that halted production. The combination of star power, controversy, and a niche distribution channel makes the film a flashpoint for both fans and critics.

## What happened
The Deadline report confirms that the full trailer for “Run Hide Fight: Infidels” is now public and that the film will premiere on the Daily Wire platform on September 16. The movie is positioned as a sequel‑type follow‑up to the 2020 Daily Wire release “Run Hide Fight,” with Kyle Rankin returning as writer‑director and a tone reminiscent of 1980s action titles such as *Red Dawn* and *Toy Soldiers*.

The trailer’s logline describes radical Islamic terrorists hijacking a liberal college’s pro‑Palestine encampment to impose Sharia law, prompting accusations of xenophobia and Islamophobia from critics, including a Los Angeles Times review that called the premise “rehashing 40‑year‑old tropes.” Production was also marred by an on‑set accident in which Jonathan Majors and co‑star JC Kilcoyne fell through an unsecured window, leading to a crew walk‑off and heightened safety concerns.

The film’s producers include Ben Shapiro of The Daily Wire and Dallas Sonnier of Bonfire Legend, among others. Majors’ involvement marks his first major role since being sidelined by major studios following a domestic‑violence case, making the trailer a notable moment in his career trajectory.
