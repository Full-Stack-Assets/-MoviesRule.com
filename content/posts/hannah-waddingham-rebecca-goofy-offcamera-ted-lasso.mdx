The fourth season of Apple TV+’s “Ted Lasso” drops on August 5, bringing back the beloved mix of optimism and sharp humor. In a candid interview, star Hannah Waddingham, who plays the scheming yet vulnerable owner Rebecca Welton, contrasted the ruthless decisions her character makes on‑screen with her own goofy, biscuit‑loving off‑camera personality. The conversation also touched on the recent loss of her longtime co‑star Anthony Head, whose presence shaped Rebecca’s on‑screen rivalry. Understanding this duality gives fans a richer view of why the show’s emotional beats land so powerfully.

<Callout type="takeaway">Rebecca’s on‑screen ruthlessness is a performance, while Waddingham’s real life is delightfully goofy.</Callout>

## What happened
The series returned after a three‑year hiatus, with season 4 premiering August 5 on Apple TV+. Hannah Waddingham confirmed she is back as Rebecca, noting the character’s evolution and the emotional weight of recent storylines, including the scene that mirrors her own father’s heart surgery.

In the interview with Marie Claire, Waddingham described herself as “the biscuit‑eating woman,” joked that the show’s famous biscuits were “rancid,” and opened up about the grief she feels after the death of Anthony Head, who played her on‑screen ex‑husband Rupert Mannion.

The same period saw the series continue its award streak, with previous seasons earning multiple Emmys and over 25 billion minutes of viewing worldwide.

## Why it matters
