The new trailer for Paloma Schneideman’s feature debut Big Girls Don’t Cry has just hit US screens, giving audiences a first look at the film’s sun‑baked New Zealand summer. Blue Fox Entertainment unveiled the clip after the movie’s festival circuit, which included Sundance, SXSW, BFI Flare, TIFF Next Wave and Melbourne. The teaser follows 14‑year‑old Sid (Ani Palmer) as she navigates desire, identity and rebellion in 2006 rural New Zealand. With executive production by Oscar‑winner Jane Campion, the film promises a fresh LGBTQ coming‑of‑age voice that could stand out in the fall slate.

## What happened
Blue Fox Entertainment released the official US trailer on August 11, 2026, accompanied by a poster that highlights the film’s pastel‑washed landscape. The movie premiered in the World Dramatic Competition at Sundance earlier this year and subsequently screened at SXSW, BFI Flare, TIFF Next Wave and the Melbourne Film Festival. It is written and directed by New Zealand filmmaker Paloma Schneideman, marking her first feature after several shorts, and is produced by Thomas Coppell and Vicky Pope.

The cast centers on newcomer Ani Palmer as Sid, with supporting roles from Rain Spencer and Noah Taylor. Executive producer Jane Campion’s involvement adds a layer of prestige, and the film is set during the summer of 2006, exploring Sid’s online curiosity, sexual awakening and the fallout of her rebellious choices. Big Girls Don’t Cry will open in limited US theaters on October 9, 2026.

## Why it matters
The film arrives at a moment when LGBTQ stories are gaining mainstream traction, yet authentic regional perspectives remain scarce. Jane Campion’s backing signals industry confidence, which could help the movie secure a streaming deal after its theatrical window. Moreover, the festival buzz suggests the film may become a touchstone for future indie projects from Oceania.
