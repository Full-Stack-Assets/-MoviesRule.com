Cyberpunk 2077’s flagship RPG is expanding beyond the screen with a new physical collectible. The Cyberpunk Quadra Turbo-R V-Tech RC set was announced for a September 4, 2026 release, priced at £149.99. The set includes a remote-controlled car that doubles as a PC gaming pad, a dedicated Jackie Welles garage display, and an integrated charging system. Fans of the game’s iconic Quadra vehicle see this as the most ambitious piece of merchandise to date, and it arrives just as the title celebrates its sixth anniversary.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Quadra RC set turns Night City’s most coveted car into a premium, dual-function collector’s item.</Callout>

## What happened
The official Bandai Namco Store revealed that the Cyberpunk Quadra Turbo-R V-Tech RC set will ship on 4 September 2026 for £149.99. The package contains a dual-purpose controller engineered to operate the vehicle as a remote-controlled car and as a PC gaming pad, a recreated Jackie Welles’ Garage serving as a display box and charging station, an integrated charging system, and a collector’s box.

Pre‑orders opened shortly after the announcement and are limited to the initial production run, reflecting the set’s status as a premium collector’s item.

## Why it matters
The release signals CD Projekt RED’s continued strategy of monetizing the Cyberpunk 2077 brand through high-end physical products, deepening fan engagement beyond the digital experience. Owning a functional replica of the Quadra Turbo‑R can enhance immersion for players and collectors, while the dual‑function controller blurs the line between toy and gaming peripheral, potentially influencing future merchandise designs.

## How to think about it
If you’re a Cyberpunk fan, weigh the set’s novelty against your budget and display space. Consider whether you’ll use the controller for gaming or mainly display it as a trophy. Purchasing early can secure a unit before the limited run sells out, but be prepared for the premium cost.

<Callout type="warning">Do not assume the RC set includes additional Cyberpunk accessories; it is a standalone collector’s package.</Callout>
