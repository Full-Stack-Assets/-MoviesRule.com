The zombie apocalypse is back on the streaming radar as Netflix announces it will host the entire Walking Dead universe. AMC Global Media and Netflix have sealed a multi‑year, co‑exclusive licensing agreement that brings all 371 episodes—from the original series to its six spin‑offs—under one roof. The deal arrives just as the franchise continues to expand with new shows and a loyal fan base hungry for binge‑watching. For subscribers, this means a single platform to revisit classic arcs and catch up on newer installments without juggling multiple services.

<Callout type="takeaway">All 371 episodes of The Walking Dead and its spin‑offs will stream exclusively on Netflix under a new multi‑year deal.</Callout>

## What happened
Netflix and AMC Global Media officially announced a multi‑year global licensing agreement that grants co‑exclusive streaming rights to every series in The Walking Dead universe. The contract covers the flagship series, which ran 11 seasons and 177 episodes, as well as all six spin‑offs, totaling 371 episodes.

The announcement came on July 30, 2026, roughly four years after the original series concluded. By bundling the entire catalog, the deal positions Netflix as the primary home for the franchise while AMC secures a long‑term revenue source.

## Why it matters
Consolidating the entire library on a single platform simplifies the viewing experience for fans and strengthens Netflix’s position in the competitive streaming market. AMC benefits from a steady licensing revenue stream and broader global exposure, while the franchise gains renewed cultural relevance as new audiences discover its legacy. However, the exclusivity may limit access for viewers who prefer other services, potentially fragmenting the fan community.

Treat the deal as an opportunity to plan a comprehensive Walking Dead marathon: start with the original series, then explore the spin‑offs in chronological order.
