The free Mortal Shell 2 beta launched today on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, riding the wave of a Steam open beta that logged over 500,000 downloads. The demo lets players experience the prologue, test eight distinct Shell classes, and earn an exclusive Flayed Harbinger skin. Because the beta is free and the full game arrives in a few weeks, the move could dramatically expand the player base and shape early expectations for the sequel.

<Callout type="takeaway">The free console beta leverages Steam momentum to boost awareness ahead of Mortal Shell 2’s launch.</Callout>

## What happened
The Steam open beta attracted more than 500,000 downloads and earned a 93% positive rating from over 2,200 English reviews, securing a "Very Positive" badge on the platform. Critics praised the sequel’s expanded world design and refined combat, noting it feels like a significant step up from the 2020 original.

Following that success, the beta was released at no cost on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. Players receive access to the prologue, known as the Marrow Keep section, and can choose from eight different Shells, each offering unique abilities. Progression through the story carries over to the full game, and participants earn the exclusive Flayed Harbinger cosmetic skin.

The beta is a standalone experience; prior knowledge of the first Mortal Shell is not required. While the core mechanics remain familiar—collecting fallen warriors' Shells to gain new playstyles—the sequel introduces a larger open-world layout and deeper narrative threads.

## Why it matters
Offering the beta for free on consoles turns a strong PC momentum into a cross‑platform buzz generator, likely pulling millions of new players into the franchise before launch. Early hands‑on feedback helps the developers fine‑tune difficulty curves and balance the new Shell abilities, reducing post‑launch patches. The exclusive skin and story‑skip incentive also reward early adopters, encouraging conversion from beta participants to full‑price purchasers.

## How to think about it
Treat the beta as a low‑risk way to sample the game's core loop and decide if the sequel’s difficulty and aesthetic resonate with you. Focus on experimenting with different Shells to understand the combat variety, and note any balance quirks that feel off. If the experience feels compelling, consider pre‑ordering the full game to retain the Flayed Harbinger skin and skip the tutorial section.

<Callout type="warning">Progress made in the beta—such as weapons, currency, and collectibles—will reset at launch; only story progression carries over.</Callout>
