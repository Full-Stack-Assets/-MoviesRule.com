The Crave Original series Heated Rivalry has confirmed its Season 2 cast with Justice Smith and Charlie Gillespie playing Harris Drover and Troy Barrett. The new season is set to premiere in spring 2027. Production on Season 2 started in Canada and will continue the story of Shane and Ilya while introducing a new couple. Justice Smith and Charlie Gillespie were chosen for their ability to bring depth and heart to their characters. 
   <Callout type="takeaway">Justice Smith and Charlie Gillespie will play the lead roles of Harris and Troy in Heated Rivalry Season 2.</Callout>
   ## What happened
   Heated Rivalry Season 1 adapted Book 2 of Rachel Reid’s Game Changers series and incorporated elements from Book 1. Season 2 will tackle Book 6, The Long Game, and introduce a new couple from Book 5, Role Model. Harris Drover is the team’s social media manager, and Troy Barrett is a hockey player with a history of bullying. 
   The executive producers stated that Justice Smith and Charlie Gillespie immediately connected with the vulnerability and resilience of their characters. 
   The new season will continue the story of Shane Hollander and Ilya Rozanov while introducing the new couple.
