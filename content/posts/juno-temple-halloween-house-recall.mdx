Juno Temple, known for Ted Lasso, surprised fans by revealing she spent her childhood in the infamous Michael Myers house from Halloween. She discussed this on Hot Ones Versus with co‑star Jason Sudeikis. The house, located in South Pasadena, has long been a pilgrimage site for horror enthusiasts. Temple recalled seeing tour buses and believing they were there for her. The revelation connects a beloved actress to a legendary horror film location, sparking fresh interest in the site.

## What happened
During a Tuesday appearance on Hot Ones Versus, Temple challenged Jason Sudeikis to guess which classic horror house she grew up in. After Sudeikis guessed Rosemary’s Baby, Temple clarified it was the South Pasadena residence used as Michael Myers’ childhood home in John Carpenter’s 1978 film Halloween.

She described how tour buses would arrive each October, announcing the location as the Michael Myers house, and she thought the attention was directed at her. Temple also mentioned sharing the story in a Ted Lasso press interview with Hits Radio UK, where co‑star Hannah Waddingham reacted with surprise.
