CBS has officially addressed the puzzling timeslot shift for Tracker's upcoming season 4. The network announced on Instagram that the series will debut on Sunday, Oct 4 at 9:30 pm ET, a half‑hour later than its usual 9 pm slot. CBS says the later start is only for the premiere and the show will revert to 9 pm for subsequent episodes. This clarification comes after fans expressed confusion on social media. Understanding the schedule helps viewers avoid missing the first episode and sets expectations for the rest of the season.

## What happened
CBS posted on its official Instagram account that the October 4 opener will air at 9:30 pm ET, not the standard 9 pm slot that the series has occupied for its first three seasons. The network emphasized that this adjustment is limited to the premiere night and that the series will settle back into its 9 pm slot for the remainder of the season.

No detailed explanation was provided, but industry observers note that a temporary shift often accommodates special programming or a lead‑in event on the same evening. Because the change affects only the first episode, it is being treated as a one‑off scheduling decision rather than a permanent move.

## Why it matters
Fans who set reminders based on the historic 9 pm slot may miss the opening episode, potentially hurting live viewership numbers that networks still track for advertising rates. The brief delay also signals how flexible broadcast schedules can be when accommodating network priorities, such as holiday specials or high‑profile sports broadcasts. For advertisers, the clarification helps align ad buys with the actual airtime, ensuring campaigns reach the intended audience.
