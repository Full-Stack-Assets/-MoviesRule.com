## What happened
Screen Rant reported that the ROM hack, titled Moonrise Regalia, was unveiled in a Reddit post by user Pontius Stone. The hack is described as a dungeon‑centric experience set in a compact overworld, diverging from the typical fan projects that simply re‑skin the original game.
The project adds nine main dungeons, four mini‑dungeons, custom puzzles, and new items such as the switch hook and cane, alongside original music tracks. Although the hack is free to download, it has already generated positive comments on YouTube, with players praising its fresh take on the Oracle of Ages formula.
