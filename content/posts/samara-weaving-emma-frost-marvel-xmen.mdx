Samara Weaving is set to join Marvel Studios' revived X‑Men universe, a move that follows Disney's 2019 acquisition of Fox's mutant franchise. Deadline reports that the actress has been officially chosen to play telepathic mutant Emma Frost in the upcoming film. The decision came after director Jake Schreier and Marvel president Kevin Feige reviewed a slate of candidates and settled on Weaving earlier this week. Weaving’s casting revives a character last seen on screen in 2011’s X‑Men: First Class and raises expectations for a fresh interpretation.

## What happened
Sources tell Deadline that after an extensive audition process, Jake Schreier and Kevin Feige selected Samara Weaving to portray Emma Frost, a telepathic mutant who debuted in the comics in the 1980s and evolved from villain to core X‑Man. The casting was confirmed in early July, and the script is being penned by Lee Sung Jin of “Beef” and Joanna Calo of “The Bear.”

Weaving arrives on the project after a standout 2026 season, most notably leading the horror‑comedy sequel Ready or Not 2: Here I Come. The sequel earned a 90% audience approval rating and grossed three times its production budget within a single day of its May 5 digital release, underscoring her box‑office draw.

The announcement arrives as Marvel Studios continues to integrate former Fox properties, with the X‑Men franchise poised to become a central pillar of its future cinematic slate.

## Why it matters
Bringing a high‑profile actress like Weaving into the fold signals Marvel’s intent to blend fresh talent with beloved comic characters, potentially attracting both longtime fans and new audiences. Emma Frost’s complex history—from supervillain to team leader—offers narrative depth that could expand the mutant mythology beyond the familiar roster. Additionally, the film’s development under Schreier and a seasoned writing duo suggests a tonal shift that may differentiate the new X‑Men from earlier iterations, influencing streaming strategies and box‑office expectations.
