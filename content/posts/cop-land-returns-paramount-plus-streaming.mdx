Paramount+ added a classic to its library today: James Mangold’s 1997 crime thriller Cop Land, starring Robert De Niro and Sylvester Stallone. The 105‑minute film, which pits a small‑town sheriff against corrupt NYPD detectives, is now available for on‑demand streaming. Its return gives fans a chance to revisit one of De Niro’s grittier performances without hunting for a DVD. With the platform also rolling out other notable titles, the move underscores Paramount+’s push to bolster its crime‑genre catalog. For viewers who enjoy 1990s thrillers, the timing couldn’t be better.

<Callout type="takeaway">Cop Land’s streaming debut revives a standout ’90s crime thriller for modern audiences.</Callout>

## What happened
Cop Land joins a fresh batch of titles on Paramount+ that includes Blade Runner 2049, Cloverfield, and The Hangover trilogy. The film, released on August 13 1997 by Miramax, earned a 75% Tomatometer rating and grossed over $63 million worldwide against a $15 million budget. It follows Sheriff Freddy Heflin (Sylvester Stallone) as he confronts corrupt NYPD officers, notably De Niro’s Detective Moe Tilden, in the small New Jersey town of Garrison.

The ensemble also features Harvey Keitel, Ray Liotta, Peter Berg, Janeane Garofalo, Robert Patrick, and Michael Rapaport. Directed by James Mangold, Cop Land showcases a gritty, character‑driven narrative that blends police procedural tension with personal moral conflict.

## Why it matters
Streaming Cop Land reintroduces a critically praised entry in De Niro’s crime‑film legacy, giving new audiences access to a story about institutional corruption that still resonates. Its addition strengthens Paramount+’s strategy to attract viewers seeking nostalgic, award‑winning thrillers, differentiating the service from competitors focused on newer releases. For fans of 1990s cinema, the film’s availability also highlights the enduring appeal of gritty, ensemble‑driven storytelling.
