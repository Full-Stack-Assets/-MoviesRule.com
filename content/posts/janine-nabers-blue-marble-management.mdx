Janine Nabers, the award‑winning writer behind Prime Video’s limited series Swarm, has just signed with Theresa Kang’s Blue Marble Management. The deal expands her representation across film, television, and all media, adding to her existing third‑overall deal with Amazon. Nabers is currently developing Shani, a scripted series based on the ’90s doll, for Amazon and Mattel. Her move to Blue Marble places her alongside a roster that includes Lena Waithe and Prentice Penny, signaling a strategic push into higher‑profile projects. Industry observers see the partnership as a signal of growing clout for creators who blend prestige TV with commercial franchises.

## What happened

Theresa Kang’s Blue Marble Management announced an exclusive signing with Janine Nabers, covering representation for film, television, and all media. Nabers, a writer, playwright, and producer, is best known for co‑creating and executive producing Prime Video’s Swarm, which earned the 2024 NAACP Image Award for Outstanding Limited Series and an Emmy nomination for writing.

The deal arrives as Nabers, currently under her third overall television agreement with Amazon, develops Shani—a series adaptation of the iconic ’90s doll for Amazon and Mattel, which would be Mattel’s first premium scripted series if greenlit. She joins a Blue Marble roster that features Lena Waithe, Prentice Penny, Gordon Smith, and Jason Kim, while still being represented by WME and Mahdi Salehi of Hansen, Jacobson, Teller.

## Why it matters

Nabers’ partnership with Blue Marble signals a consolidation of talent that can navigate both prestige awards‑season content and brand‑driven franchises, giving her projects stronger negotiating power. For Amazon, the move reinforces its commitment to nurturing creators who can deliver critically acclaimed series and commercially viable properties. The addition also boosts Blue Marble’s credibility as a boutique agency capable of attracting top‑tier writers, potentially reshaping representation dynamics in the streaming era.
