Kara Robinson Chamberlain, a kidnapping survivor, is now a true crime investigator in the new series Somebody Knows Something. The series premieres on August 10 on Freeform, with two back-to-back episodes. Chamberlain was abducted in 2002 and escaped, later exposing a serial killer. Now, she is reopening unsolved murders and missing persons cases in this eight-part series. 
   <Callout type="takeaway">Somebody Knows Something is a true crime series that follows Kara Robinson Chamberlain as she investigates unsolved cases.</Callout>
   ## What happened
   The series premiere investigates the cases of Sabrina Aisenberg and Lashaya Stine, who vanished in 1997 and 2016, respectively. The first episode looks into the case of Sabrina Aisenberg, who disappeared at five months old. The second episode investigates the disappearance of Lashaya Stine, who was a teenager when she vanished. 
   The series will air weekly on Mondays at 10 p.m. PT/ET on Freeform, with episodes available to stream on Hulu and Disney+ the next day. 
   Somebody Knows Something is available to stream live on services that carry Freeform, including DirecTV, Fubo, Sling, and Hulu + Live TV.
   ## Why it matters
   The series is significant because it sheds light on unsolved cases and provides a platform for the families of the victims to share their stories. It also highlights the importance of investigative journalism and the role that true crime series can play in bringing attention to cold cases. 
   The series is also notable for its unique approach, with Chamberlain, a survivor of a similar crime, taking an active role in the investigation.
