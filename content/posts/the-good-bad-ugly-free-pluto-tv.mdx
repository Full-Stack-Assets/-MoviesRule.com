The legendary spaghetti Western The Good, the Bad and the Ugly has just become free to stream, and the news comes with a seal of approval from Quentin Tarantino. Pluto TV added the 1966 Sergio Leone classic to its ad‑supported catalog for August 2026, joining a small batch of free Westerns. The timing lines up with the film’s 60th anniversary, giving a new generation a chance to see what Tarantino calls the “greatest achievement in cinema history.” With no subscription required, the move could reshape how classic cinema reaches casual viewers.

## What happened
Pluto TV announced that The Good, the Bad and the Ugly will be available on its free, ad‑supported platform throughout August 2026. The film, directed by Sergio Leone and starring Clint Eastwood, Lee Van Cleef and Eli Wallach, celebrates its 60th birthday this year. Tarantino has repeatedly called the movie the best‑directed film of all time and the greatest achievement in cinema, a endorsement that has kept the title in cultural conversations.

The addition arrives alongside two other Tarantino‑approved Westerns—Terrence Malick’s Days of Heaven and Robert Altman’s McCabe and Mrs. Miller—both also streaming for free on Pluto TV this month. Collider’s coverage notes that the service is expanding its classic‑film lineup, positioning itself as a go‑to destination for budget‑conscious cinephiles.

## Why it matters
Making a seminal work of the genre free removes a financial barrier that has kept many younger viewers away from classic cinema. Tarantino’s public admiration acts as a cultural catalyst, often prompting spikes in viewership and renewed scholarly interest. For Pluto TV, the move showcases how free, ad‑supported platforms can compete with subscription services by leveraging iconic titles and influencer endorsement to attract and retain audiences.
