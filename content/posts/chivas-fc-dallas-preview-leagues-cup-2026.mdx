Chivas de Guadalajara faces FC Dallas on Saturday night at PayPal Park in San Jose, California, as the second match of Leagues Cup 2026 Group Stage. The Mexican side comes off a 1‑1 draw with LAFC that left them with a single point, while Dallas arrived with a 2‑0 win over Querétaro and three points. Both clubs are eager to secure a victory that could determine who advances to the next round. The matchup also revives a brief rivalry that dates back to a 2007 SuperLiga tie and a 2008 friendly.

## What happened
In their opening group game, Chivas drew 1‑1 with Los Ángeles FC, with Roberto “Piojo” Alvarado scoring the equaliser. The match went to a penalty shoot‑out, which LAFC won, leaving Chivas with one point.

FC Dallas opened its campaign by beating Querétaro 2‑0, thanks to goals from Santiago Moreno and Joaquín Valiente, earning three points. The upcoming fixture marks the third encounter between the clubs: a 1‑1 draw in the 2007 SuperLiga and a 2‑1 Dallas win in a 2008 friendly.

## Why it matters
A win for Chivas would lift them from the bottom of the group and keep their hopes of advancing alive, while a loss could see them eliminated after just two games. Dallas, already on three points, can solidify its position at the top and avoid a tougher knockout draw. The result also influences fan engagement and broadcast interest, as both markets are keen on seeing a Mexican side succeed in the inaugural 2026 tournament.
