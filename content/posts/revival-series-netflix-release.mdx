Syfy’s acclaimed supernatural horror show Revival is moving to Netflix, with all ten episodes of season 1 set to stream on August 24, 2026. Despite solid critical praise and a dedicated fan following, Revival season 2 has not yet been greenlit, leaving the show’s future uncertain. The move to Netflix may be the key to securing a second season, as the streaming giant has a history of reviving cancelled shows. Revival’s creators are urging fans to watch and share the show to demonstrate its popularity and increase the chances of a season 2 renewal.

   Revival debuted in 2025 to solid critical praise and quickly built a fan following thanks to an initial airing on Syfy, followed by a successful streaming run on Peacock. However, despite its success, Revival season 2 has not yet been greenlit, and the show seems in some danger of never returning. The show’s creators, Aaron B.
