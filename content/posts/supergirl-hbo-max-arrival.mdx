## What happened
The announcement, posted on IMDb’s news feed, confirms that HBO Max has secured the streaming rights to the full Supergirl series. No exact launch date was given, but the wording suggests a rollout in the final quarter of 2026. The deal follows a series of negotiations that saw the show move between broadcast, cable, and streaming homes over the past decade.

Industry analysts note that the addition aligns with HBO Max’s strategy to strengthen its DC Universe offerings, complementing existing titles like "Titans" and "Doom Patrol." The platform will likely promote the series alongside other upcoming superhero releases to attract both nostalgic viewers and new audiences.
